Parameter Reference
Parameter Default Sandy loam Role in soil fluxes
qSurfScale 50.0 — Exponential scaling of infiltrating area. Higher → more infiltration → less runoff. Lower → more runoff even when soil is dry.
k_soil 9.4×10⁻⁶ 1–5×10⁻⁵ Saturated hydraulic conductivity. Sets ceiling on infiltration rate (Green-Ampt) and drainage flux.
theta_sat 0.516 0.38–0.44 Porosity. Sets total storage capacity and bounds the van Genuchten K(θ) function.
theta_res 0.027 0.05–0.08 Residual water content. Lower bound on plant-available water; affects K at dry end and ET stress.
vGn_alpha −0.84 −4.0 – −1.5 Van Genuchten capillary length scale. Controls air-entry point; shifts where K begins to decline with drying.
vGn_n 1.30 1.35–1.70 Van Genuchten pore-size distribution. Higher n → steeper K(θ) falloff → faster drainage when wet, near-zero when dry.
rootingDepth 6.876 ≤ 1.0 m Depth of root zone. Controls ET draw-down volume and fracCap denominator in qSurfScale calculation. Bound to soil column depth.
rootDistExp 1.0 0.1–2.0 Shape of root density profile. Higher → roots concentrated near surface; lower → more uniform distribution with depth.
critSoilTranspire 0.175 0.10–0.30 θ above which transpiration is unstressed (NoahType). Higher → ET shuts down earlier → more residual storage.
critSoilWilting 0.075 — θ at wilting point. Below this, transpiration ceases. Lower bound on ET stress function.
wettingFrontSuction 0.30 0.10–0.30 Green-Ampt capillary suction. Higher → more infiltration capacity into dry soil → less infiltration-excess runoff.
kAnisotropic 1.0 — drainage only — Direct linear multiplier on drainage flux. Values <1 restrict drainage independently of soil moisture state.
k_macropore 0.001 — drainage only — Fast-path conductivity above theta_mp. At default, dominates drainage near saturation. Set = k_soil to disable.
theta_mp 0.401 — drainage only — Moisture threshold activating macropore flow. Set = theta_sat to disable macropore contribution.
f_impede 2.0 — Ice impedance exponent. Reduces K when soil ice is present. Relevant in cold seasons only.
soilIceScale / soilIceCV 0.13 / 0.45 — Gamma distribution parameters for frozen area fraction. Controls how much of basin is impermeable during freeze events.
compactedDepth 1.0 — drainage only (pow_prof) — Anchors Ksat depth-decay. At default = column depth, has no effect on 1m column.
